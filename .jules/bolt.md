## 2026-09-19 - Intl Formatter Instantiation in Render Loops\n**Learning:** This app architecture completely redraws the main content via `innerHTML` on every interaction. Creating new `Intl.DateTimeFormat` or `Intl.NumberFormat` instances inside loops (like generating 364 days for heatmaps) causes a severe performance bottleneck because the `Intl` constructors are very expensive.\n**Action:** Always cache `Intl.*` formatter instances by locale/timezone/options to prevent repeated instantiation during redrawing.
